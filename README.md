# Unscroll 🌿

> **Distraction-Free Social App · People, not feeds.**  
> Zero algorithmic reels, zero infinite scrolling, zero explore recommendations.

Live Web App: **[https://mahimadin.github.io/UnScroll/](https://mahimadin.github.io/UnScroll/)**

---

## ✨ Features

- **24-Hour Ephemeral Stories**: Share photos and moments that naturally disappear after 24 hours. Full-screen tap viewer with viewer lists and direct DM replies.
- **Direct 1-on-1 Messaging**: Clean chat with photo and isolated short video sharing.
- **Isolated Video Playback**: Videos sent in chat open in a self-contained player with strict boundaries—no algorithmic next-video scrolling.
- **Followers & Privacy Control**: Choose between public or private accounts with manual follow request approvals.
- **"Not Following Back" Tracker**: Instantly see which Unscroll accounts you follow who do not follow you back.
- **Client-Side Instagram Follower Audit**:
  - Upload `followers_1.json` and `following.json` from your official Meta / Instagram data download.
  - 100% private & client-side in your browser: zero passwords, zero login, zero API limits.
  - Categorizes: **Not Following Back**, **Fans** (who follow you but you don't follow back), and **Mutuals**.
  - 1-click links to Instagram profiles with safety batching checkboxes (30–50/day).
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable directly onto Android & iOS home screens for full-screen offline-ready native app experience.

## 📱 How to Install on Mobile (Android & iOS)

### Android (Chrome / Edge / Samsung Internet):
1. Open **[https://mahimadin.github.io/UnScroll/](https://mahimadin.github.io/UnScroll/)** on your phone's browser.
2. Tap the **three dots menu (⋮)** in the top right.
3. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. The app icon will appear on your phone's home screen and app drawer, launching full-screen without browser address bars!

### iOS (Safari):
1. Open **[https://mahimadin.github.io/UnScroll/](https://mahimadin.github.io/UnScroll/)** in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.

## 📦 Converting into Standalone APK (1-Click)

Whenever you want to generate a standalone signed `.apk` file from this Web App:

1. Open [PWABuilder.com](https://www.pwabuilder.com/).
2. Enter your live URL: `https://mahimadin.github.io/UnScroll/`.
3. Click **Start** → PWABuilder analyzes the manifest and service worker.
4. Click **Package for Android** → Download the generated Android APK / AAB package.
